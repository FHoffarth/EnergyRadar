EnergyRadar macOS beta limitations

- This artifact is not notarized and is not stapled.
- Unless ARCHITECTURE-AUDIT.txt says Developer ID signed, normal double-click may be blocked by Gatekeeper.
- Control-click -> Open may be required for an unsigned or ad-hoc signed beta.
- Removing quarantine attributes is a last-resort diagnostic step, not the normal installation path.
- CI performs a non-interactive packaged process smoke; it does not prove complete GUI behavior.
- Physical Apple Silicon and Intel Mac results must be recorded independently.
- No Universal 2 bundle is included.
- A stable public release still requires Developer ID Application signing, hardened runtime, notarization, and stapling.
