EnergyRadar macOS physical hardware smoke checklist

Artifact identity
[ ] Verify the ZIP SHA-256 against the sidecar checksum.
[ ] Extract the ZIP without CRC errors.
[ ] Move EnergyRadar.app to /Applications.
[ ] Confirm the tested app reports the commit and architecture in BUILDINFO.txt.

Launch and platform integration
[ ] Record the first-launch Gatekeeper message.
[ ] If needed, use Control-click -> Open and record the result.
[ ] Confirm a visible, non-blank EnergyRadar window.
[ ] Confirm the process architecture with Activity Monitor or `file`/`ps`.
[ ] Confirm Apple Silicon runs without Rosetta, or Intel runs natively as x86_64.
[ ] Confirm QtWebEngineProcess launches without a separate Dock icon.
[ ] Confirm the EnergyRadar Dock icon is correct.

Application behavior
[ ] Open Jetzt, Heute, Geräte, Gedächtnis, and Einstellungen.
[ ] Verify Settings Save/Discard and persistence after restart.
[ ] Verify charts, history, weather, and Solar Economy render without a blank view.
[ ] Exercise Fronius and SmartMeter connection checks with authorized test devices.
[ ] Exercise folder picker, export folder, log file/folder opening, and mail handoff.
[ ] Quit cleanly and reopen cleanly.
[ ] Confirm no file below EnergyRadar.app changed during use.

Record every unchecked or failed item. CI startup smoke is not a substitute for this checklist.
